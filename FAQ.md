# Frequently asked questions (and answers!)

## Is this the entire Angular Boot Camp curriculum?

This is the bulk of the example code we use in Angular Boot Camp, but
there are extra parts that we distribute or build live during training.
Reading through these files is not a substitute for attending class;
much of the "magic" is from the instruction and workshop assistance.

## Why have you published this material?

* We’ve always made the examples we use during class available for
  students to continue accessing after training is concluded. This just
  makes that process easier, both for the students and for us.

* This is a great way for potential students to preview the kinds of
  topics we cover and get a sense of how they're presented.

* Posting here makes it straightforward to load the examples in [GitHub
  Codespaces](https://github.com/features/codespaces) or similar tools.

* The web is full of information about Angular, but it varies widely in
  quality and timeliness. By publishing this material, we hope to offer
  a reliable source of up-to-date examples of numerous Angular features.

## Will these examples be updated?

Yes, absolutely. Angular continues to grow and improve, and we're always refining how we teach Angular Boot Camp, so these examples are regularly updated to keep up with the changes. Check back often!

## How can I help?

Link, post, star this repository, and encourage your friends and
coworkers to attend [Angular Boot Camp](https://angularbootcamp.com/)!
